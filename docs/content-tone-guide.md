# Content Tone Guide

## Tone Characteristics

The writing should feel:
- emotionally intelligent
- observant
- warm
- playful
- intentional
- personal

Avoid:
- generic compliments
- overly dramatic writing
- forced romance
- cringe internet poetry

---

# Writing Style

Strong writing qualities:
- specificity
- observation
- subtlety
- emotional precision
- conversational warmth

---

# Humor Style

Humor should feel:
- affectionate
- intelligent
- slightly teasing
- observational

Avoid:
- excessive memes
- random internet humor
- chaotic Gen-Z overload

---

# Emotional Writing Philosophy

The emotional impact should come from:
- accuracy
- observation
- understanding

Not exaggerated language.

---

# Important Rule

The experience should consistently feel:
"Made specifically for Annie."

Personal details matter more than poetic complexity.
