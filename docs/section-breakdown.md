# Section Breakdown

## Section 1 — Intro / Landing

Purpose:
Create curiosity, atmosphere, and emotional anticipation.

Possible elements:
- leaves
- trees
- parallax movement
- ambient sound
- cinematic reveal

The opening should feel magical but subtle.

The reveal moment is emotionally important.

---

## Section 2 — Things Annie Says

Purpose:
Build familiarity, humor, and emotional recognition.

Structure ideas:
- floating quote cards
- drifting dialogue fragments
- observational commentary

Tone:
- playful
- intelligent
- affectionate

---

## Section 3 — 50 Things I Love About Annie

Purpose:
Main emotional appreciation section.

Structure:
- interactive grid/cards
- expandable overlays
- animated reveals

Important:
Mix emotional, funny, observant, and oddly specific entries.

Avoid repetitive compliment structures.

---

## Section 4 — Moments I'll Never Forget

Purpose:
Nostalgia and emotional memory.

Structure ideas:
- floating polaroids
- cinematic memory cards
- layered image storytelling

Tone:
- intimate
- reflective
- warm

---

## Section 5 — Did You Know?

Purpose:
Humor and emotional relief.

This section should feel:
- chaotic
- playful
- interactive

Possible use of:
- flash cards
- fake scientific documentation
- funny observations
- "Annie lore"

Potential Michael Jackson reference:
"Annie, are you okay?"

---

## Section 6 — Letter To Annie

Purpose:
Final emotional conclusion.

Structure:
- envelope interaction
- unfolding animation
- letter reveal
- typing effect

The ending should feel:
- quiet
- sincere
- emotionally honest
- intimate

Avoid excessive melodrama.
