# Section Breakdown

## Section 1 — Intro / Landing

Purpose:
Create curiosity, atmosphere, and emotional anticipation.

Possible elements:
- leaves
- trees
- parallax movement
- ambient sound
- cinematic reveal

The opening should feel magical but subtle.

The reveal moment is emotionally important.

---

## Section 2 — 25 Things I Love About Annie

Purpose:
Main emotional appreciation section.

Structure:
- interactive grid/cards
- expandable overlays
- animated reveals

Important:
Mix emotional, funny, observant, and oddly specific entries.

Avoid repetitive compliment structures.

---

## Section 3 — Moments and Phrases

Purpose:
Combine humor, nostalgia, and emotional recognition through memorable moments and iconic phrases.

Structure:
- Card stacking effect with 3 sticky cards
- Left column: tall image; Right column: descriptive text
- GSAP-powered scale-down as cards scroll past

Cards:
1. "When she collected extra sheet during calculus" — playful calculus exam moment
2. "She showed up" — heartland rescue story
3. "Impudence of twerking maggotas" — signature phrase

Tone:
- playful
- nostalgic
- intimate
- affectionate humor

---

## Section 4 — Did You Know?

Purpose:
Humor and emotional relief.

This section should feel:
- chaotic
- playful
- interactive

Possible use of:
- flash cards
- fake scientific documentation
- funny observations
- "Annie lore"

Potential Michael Jackson reference:
"Annie, are you okay?"

---

## Section 5 — Letter To Annie

Purpose:
Final emotional conclusion.

Structure:
- envelope interaction
- unfolding animation
- letter reveal
- typing effect

The ending should feel:
- quiet
- sincere
- emotionally honest
- intimate

Avoid excessive melodrama.
